# Changelog

## [v2.0.0] - 2026-11-04
### Major Mathematical, Algorithmic, and Statistical Upgrades
- **Unified Simulation Architecture (`simulation_core.py`)**: Consolidated all mathematical equations, stochastic integrators, graph-pruning algorithms, and survival analyses into a single modular library imported by all figure scripts and Jupyter notebooks.
- **Endogenous Hebbian Plasticity vs. Ablation Control (`Figure2_Kuramoto_MainDynamics.py`)**: Replaced hard-coded `K_schedule` with endogenous co-evolution of inter-population coupling $K_{\text{cross},ij}(t)$ driven by transient external task entrainment $I_{\text{ext}}(t)$, paired with a `Plasticity OFF` ablation control and a partial-cue recall assay ($t \in [135, 145]$ a.u.).
- **Operational Von Neumann Disruptive Readout (`Figure 2F`)**: Implemented physical phase-probing readout perturbation demonstrating unavoidable transient Hebbian depotentiation ($\Delta K = -0.083\text{ a.u.}, P = 1.4 \times 10^{-22}$).
- **Strict Monotonic Developmental Pruning & Maslov–Sneppen Null Model (`Figure 2C–D`)**: Implemented strict edge-subset elimination ($E(t_2) \subset E(t_1)$) reproducing empirical mouse hippocampal CA3 sparsification ($6.37\% \to 2.79\% \to 2.17\%, R^2 = 1.000$), Watts–Strogatz binary and Onnela weighted clustering against Erdős–Rényi 95% CIs, and disynaptic motif enrichment against 300 degree-preserving Maslov–Sneppen null networks ($Z = +10.85, P = 0.0033$).
- **Consolidated Attractor First-Passage Escape & Stochastic Resonance ITE (`Figure 2G–H`)**: Initialized escape trials directly inside the consolidated attractor ($\sigma_c = 0.70$) and implemented operational subthreshold cued Information Transfer Efficiency (ITE), revealing a stochastic-resonance peak at $\sigma = 0.40$ ($\text{ITE} = 0.145$).
- **Exact Kuramoto Potential Energy Wells, $2 \times 2$ Factorial Survival Design, and Kramers Scaling (`Figure 3` & `Figure S2`)**: Computed exact potential wells $E(\psi) = -\frac{1}{2N}\sum A_{ij}\cos(\theta_j - \theta_i)$, analyzed right-censored escape times via Kaplan–Meier Restricted Mean Survival Time (RMST) across a $2 \times 2$ factorial design ($\rho \times K_0$) plus a Matched Total Synaptic Budget control ($K_0 \cdot \rho = 43.4$), and verified Kramers escape rate scaling ($\ln \tau_{\text{RMST}} = -3.56 + 12.15/\sigma^2, R^2 = 0.8507, P = 0.0257$).
- **Multi-Seed Sensitivity Grid, Global PRCC, and Interleaved Replay (`Figure S1`)**: Evaluated a $7 \times 7$ $(\eta, \lambda_K)$ grid across 10 matched seeds per cell (490 runs), global Partial Rank Correlation Coefficients (PRCC) across 200 Latin-hypercube samples, and 4-pattern sequential learning with sleep-like interleaved replay.
